# Clear patch — production spec

One patch for the whole line: tee, cap, tote, and anything worn that comes later.

| | |
|---|---|
| Size | 63.5 × 63.5 mm (2.5 in) square |
| Corners | Square. Use a **laser-cut (hot-cut) edge** with a satin-stitch border, not a merrowed edge — a merrow border forces rounded corners. |
| Construction | Embroidered, 100% thread coverage, on twill backing |
| Border | Satin stitch, 3 mm wide, mitred at the corners |
| Mark | Centred, 43 mm across. The ring and small ring in satin stitch running across the stroke; the centre dot and bar in fill or satin |
| Finest details | Small ring stroke 2.1 mm · bar 2.2 mm · hole in the small ring 4.8 mm — all above the usual 1 mm satin minimum |
| Attachment | Sewn on (running stitch just inside the border). No iron-on: it peels after washes. |
| Artwork | `clear-patch-ink.svg`, `clear-patch-land.svg`, `clear-patch-paper.svg` (true size, in mm) |

## Colourways

Thread is matched by eye to the brand hex values; the supplier picks the nearest stock thread and sends a strike-off to approve.

| Colourway | Ground | Mark and border | Goes on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ink | #16211D | #DFE3DE | Natural or paper-coloured garments, the tote |
| Land | #6B7F5C | #ECEFE9 | Light garments, the cap if it is not land-green |
| Paper | #DFE3DE | #16211D | Dark garments: the ink tee, a land-green cap |

## Placement

- **Mock-neck tees (short and long sleeve), turtleneck, crew:** wearer's left chest, centre about 10 cm (4 in) from the centre front and 18–19 cm (7–7.5 in) below the high point of the shoulder.
- **Hoodie:** centre front, between the drawcords, centre about 11 cm (4.25 in) below the neck opening. On the left chest the cord would hang across it.
- **Cap:** centred on the front panels.
- **Tote:** centred on one side, upper third.
- **Beanie (if made):** centred on the cuff; the cuff must be at least 70 mm deep for a 63.5 mm patch, or use a 50 mm version.

## Before ordering

Ask for a physical strike-off of each colourway and check: the ring reads as one clean curve, the small ring's hole stays open, and the border corners are square.
