01Two things, kept apart

The land and the house are not the same purchase.

Most of a home's price is the ground under it. Separate the two, and what is left has a cost you can point at.

The landHeld by every member
Held in common. Never sold.

Appreciation stays in the commons and every member holds a share of it, so members and investors are never reaching for the same dollar.

The houseYours to hold title to
Yours, at cost plus 15%.

Unlike land, a house has a cost you can point at. It is also why the price sits below market with no subsidy, no covenant and no income test.

ClearPath is the housing program of the Clear cooperative.

04The homes

A porch at both ends.

Permitted, code-compliant California dwellings built from panelised kits and finished by local trades. The community owns the solar and storage, and residents buy their power from it.

05Before the communities

Backyards first, because they are available now.

A master-planned community is an eighteen-to-thirty-six month problem. A backyard is not. The co-op leases them from homeowners and places relocatable studios and one-bedrooms on them.

How contributions work
06The gate

The path to a deed is visible from your first deposit.

Your deposit is your membership share. Every gate on the way unlocks something real, and none of it is paid back.

You sign, and the deed is yours.

15,000

equity credits, and you sign for the deed to your home

Day oneCycle assurance

Two grace cycles a year, from your first deposit.

1,500Home repair assurance

Repairs to the home you live in, covered by the co-op.

4,000Income assurance

If your income drops, your limit holds and credits keep vesting.

8,000Deposit assurance

The co-op covers a deposit on a new place, paid to the landlord.

15,000Rent assurance and your deed

Two months' rent on job loss, and you sign your ELPA and your Clear Deed.

The land stays in the commons, and the house is yours to hold title to outright. You can see where you are, and the date you are on track for, from the screen where you save.
