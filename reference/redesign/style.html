07Motion

Motion shows what changed. It never hides what is there.

Four moves, used everywhere. With reduced motion, each one shows its end state instead.

The riseHero headlines, once
Words rise into place.

Each word lifts out of a padded mask, and the mask comes off when it lands, so no descender or edge is ever clipped.

The scrubTied to scroll
Scroll position · 0%

Fills, rails and month strips follow the scroll, so the reader controls the pace and can go back.

The traceLight along a line
Live: green
Quiet: faded cobalt

A short lit segment runs once around an outline, then rests. It follows the thing it outlines when the layout wraps.

The countFigures that change
$30,000

A figure counts to its new value with tabular numerals, so the width never jumps.

RulesGSAP and ScrollTrigger
  • Nothing animates that a reader needs before it finishes. Content is complete with scripts off.
  • One ease for everything: a fast start that settles (cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)).
  • Loops pause when they scroll out of view, and run only on things that are actually live.
  • With reduced motion, show every end state: the final figure, the drawn line, the result of the vote.
