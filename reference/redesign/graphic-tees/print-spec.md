# Graphic Tees: print spec

Blank: the Logo Tee (CLR-W01) in Black, Cobalt and White, sizes S–XXL. Clear patch moved to the wearer’s right sleeve (centre 10 cm below the shoulder seam, square to the sleeve).
All three prints share one standard: the same printed area (about 1,250 cm² on M). Carousel Mall and Devore are centred with their top edge 10 cm below the front neck seam; Two Ten Overpass sits off to the wearer’s left, its right edge 3.5 cm in from the side seam, top edge 6 cm below the neck seam. One screen size runs S–XXL.

| Design | Print size (W × H) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Carousel Mall | 37.9 × 33.0 cm | Photo with the title and number blocks running down its sides. |
| Two Ten Overpass | 26.7 × 44.0 cm | Tall layout: photo turned on its side, outlined title and text column to its left. |
| Devore | 35.3 × 35.4 cm | Square photo framed by a ring of text. |

Corner block (all three): “SAN BERNARDINO” and the location’s coordinates, about 7 cm wide, wearer’s left front, just above the hem stitching.

Ink: full colour photos with a white underbase on Black and Cobalt; on White the type, boxes and rules print black (the boxes’ letters stay knocked out, so the tee shows through) and the photos print without underbase. Separate art for dark and white tees is in print-artwork/. Quote screen print vs DTG at the run size.

Open before printing: final artwork files (the art here was lifted from mockups and carries their wrinkle shadows), the body copy and photo credits (still placeholder text), the full coordinates, and the licence for each photograph.
