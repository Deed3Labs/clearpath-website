01The split

The land is why this works for both sides.

Member equity accrues in the structure. Appreciation accrues to the land, which backs construction borrowing as the portfolio grows.

04The downsides

The honest downsides.

The same five as a single property, and none is softened because the contribution is larger.

Five costs, and one moreRead before you sign
  1. 01LiquidityShares are illiquid compared with cash.
  2. 02ControlYou do not control the properties.
  3. 03PaperworkYou get a K-1, which arrives later than a 1099, and your accountant will charge for it.
  4. 04Deferred taxThe deferred gain still exists, and comes due if you ever sell the shares.
  5. 05ConcentrationRisk moves from your one building to a fund. Usually better, but a change, not a removal.
  6. 06One corridorEarly cohorts sit in one corridor by design, because that is what makes the merchant and housing sides compound. Anyone underwriting this should price that.
